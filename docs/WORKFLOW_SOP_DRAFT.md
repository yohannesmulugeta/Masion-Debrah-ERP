# Maison Debrah ERP — order-to-handover procedure

**Status:** Draft for staff review. The steps below describe the browser-local demo and approved process direction. They are not authorization to enter real transactions.

1. **Sales confirms the order.** Record the customer, agreed item, price, and delivery plan. If stock is in another room, arrange a transfer. If it is unavailable, do not promise immediate delivery.
2. **Finance records any deposit.** Sales may then reserve available stock and enter both a customer review date and the agreed collection/delivery date. Staff discuss an unresolved deposit reservation around day 3–5. An extension requires new dates and a reason; no refund happens automatically.
3. **Finance records the final payment.** Check the amount and unique payment reference. No item may leave while any order balance remains. Sales cannot record payments in the demo.
4. **Stock staff prepare and dispatch each item.** Verify the exact SKU, condition, quantity, source area, intended recipient, and destination. Record the dispatch. The system deducts that quantity once and leaves other order items open. This is the demo's stock-posting point, subject to final business confirmation for the live system.
5. **Delivery staff obtain signed proof.** Record the actual signatory and signed delivery document reference. Only then does that delivery show as accepted. The physical signed document is not stored by the demo.
6. **If delivery fails, record the reason.** The item remains out of sellable stock while away from the building. When it physically returns, Stock receives it into quarantine for inspection; it does not become available for sale automatically.
7. **If the customer and Finance agree on a pre-dispatch refund,** Finance records the full transaction reference and reason, cancels the order, and releases its reservations. After any dispatch, the demo stops automatic refunds and requires case review. The stated no-refund and no-voluntary-return/warranty terms need management and local legal review before live enforcement. Staff should still record complaints and defects.

The demo role picker, browser storage, and audit list are presentation tools. The live ERP needs named staff accounts, database permissions, atomic stock and finance operations, durable evidence, and a reviewed opening data import.
